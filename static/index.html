What this summary can and cannot tell you

These rules summarize trailing price behavior, not a personalized buy/sell recommendation or a confidence score. Trend compares the latest close with SMA 50 and EMA 20; momentum checks agreement between MACD histogram and 10-observation return. Volatility uses the previous 252 available readings (minimum 60); the top/bottom quartile labels are descriptive. Forecast evidence uses available resolved 20-session targets for this ticker, with a minimum of 20 origins. Targets overlap and the simulations use retrospectively adjusted data. Financial statements, news, liquidity, fees, taxes, and your investment horizon are not evaluated here.

How to read these indicators

BB uses a 20-observation mean ± two population standard deviations. SMA averages 50 observations; EMA uses a 20-observation seed and exponential smoothing. RSI uses Wilder’s 14-period smoothing (30/70 are reference levels). MACD subtracts EMA 26 from EMA 12, with an EMA 9 signal. ROC is the percentage change over 10 observations. Volatility is the 20-return sample standard deviation of log returns, annualized with √252.

All calculations use available validated dividend- and split-adjusted closes, with full-history warm-up. Missing observations are not filled; periods count available observations. These describe price behavior, not reliable buy/sell instructions. Indicator guide ↗
